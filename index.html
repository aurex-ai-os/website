Frequently Asked Questions

If it's BYOK, why shouldn't I just use a free open-source agent like OpenClaude?

+

You absolutely can, if you love managing Python environments, debugging dependency conflicts, and reading terminal outputs. We built Aurex for people who want the power of a local AI agent wrapped in a stunning, stable, native desktop UI. Aurex gives you our proprietary Model Router, a visual RAG file manager, and the native Trust Layer (approval GUI) out-of-the-box. You are paying for the execution, the safety, and the seamless workflow.

Does Aurex store my files, or train its AI on my codebase and study notes?

+

Absolutely not. Aurex is a local-first OS. Your files, knowledge graphs, and RAG databases live entirely on your local hard drive. We have no servers storing your data, meaning we physically cannot read your files or train models on them. Furthermore, because you use your own API keys (BYOK), providers like OpenAI and Anthropic are legally bound by their API terms not to train on your data.

Do I need an expensive PC or a massive GPU to run Aurex?

+

No. Aurex itself is extremely lightweight. Because you are using API keys (BYOK) to connect to Claude or OpenAI, their massive supercomputers do the heavy lifting. Aurex just orchestrates the context and UI locally. It runs perfectly on a standard MacBook Air or a basic Windows laptop. (Note: If you choose to route tasks to local offline models via Ollama, a dedicated GPU is recommended but totally optional).

If I’m paying for Aurex and my own API keys, won't this get expensive?

+

Actually, BYOK is mathematically cheaper than standard AI subscriptions. Most users spend less than $2 to $5 a month on API costs. Why? Because Aurex uses our proprietary Model Fabric Router. It automatically routes simple tasks (like organizing files or generating flashcards) to incredibly cheap models (like GPT-4o-mini or Llama 3), and only spends your pennies on expensive models (like Claude 3.5 Sonnet) when you ask it to write complex code.

I’ve seen AI agents hallucinate. Can Aurex delete my files or ruin my codebase?

+

No. This is exactly why we built the Trust Layer. Aurex operates in a strict sandbox. Before it executes any destructive terminal command, modifies a file, or pushes code, the UI pauses and presents you with a visual diff of its plan. It physically cannot execute the action until you click the [Approve] button. You have absolute control.

When public V1.0 launches, the introductory rate will end and Pro will increase to $12/month. By subscribing now, your early adopter price is locked in for the life of your account.
