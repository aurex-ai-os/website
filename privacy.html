Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

What stays on your computer

Your files, notes, search indexes, conversations and API keys are stored on your device. Aurex does not upload them to our servers. When you send a message, it goes from your computer to the AI provider you chose, using your own key. That provider handles it under its own terms.

What we collect

  • Your email address, if you join the waitlist (stored with our database provider, Supabase).
  • License information when you buy: your email, license key and a hashed device identifier, used to activate the app and enforce device limits.
  • Messages you send through the contact form, including the optional email address you give us so we can reply.
  • Order details are handled by our payment provider, Lemon Squeezy. We do not see your card number.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your prompts, your files, your API keys or your conversations.

Third-party services on this website

The site loads fonts and scripts from Google Fonts, jsDelivr, unpkg and Tailwind CDN, and uses Supabase for the waitlist. These services may receive your IP address when the page loads.

Your choices

Email us to see, correct or delete the data we hold about you, including removal from the waitlist.

Questions? Use the contact form.
