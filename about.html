About Aurex

Aurex is a private AI desktop workspace. You bring your own API keys, your files stay on your computer, and the agent asks before it changes anything.

Who builds it

Aurex is built by an independent developer. Write to us any time and a person will answer.

Why it exists

Many AI tools charge a flat monthly fee, cut you off with usage limits, or hide what they do on your computer. Aurex shows what each message costs, lets you use any model, and pauses for your approval before risky actions.

Where we are

Aurex is in a 3-month beta. Some features on the roadmap are planned and not yet released, and the site says which is which.
