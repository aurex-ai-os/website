Refund Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Founder's Lifetime

You can request a full refund within 30 days of purchase, for any reason.

Subscriptions

When Student and Pro subscriptions launch, you can request a refund within 14 days of your first payment.

How to ask

Use the contact form, choose "Refund request", and include the email you used to buy. Refunds are issued to your original payment method through Lemon Squeezy. A refunded license is deactivated.
